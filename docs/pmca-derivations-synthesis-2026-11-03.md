## 1. What the run actually was, and its verified state

Twenty derivations of the entire PMCA, each built from the same private compiled blueprint, each varying one named axis, each forced to answer the same eight problems (P1 wolf · P2 tag-vs-paraphrase · P3 unstored span · P4 harm + conscious choice · P5 new word · P6 residual tie · P7 bounded self-fix · P8 cross-base) as deterministic traces. The point, in Jonathan's words: twenty different systems means twenty different possible answers to problems — a possibility space, not a vote. **No averaging was performed anywhere, and none is performed here.** Where dossiers agree, that is recorded as convergence; where they split, the split is carded for ruling, not smoothed.

Verified state of the set (Gauge, checked against the files on disk, repairs disk-verified by Alder):

- **20/20 dossiers complete.** D17 is the only one complete with no invariant bend — its claim was tested hardest and held. Fifteen are complete with their bends named OPEN. Four were incomplete as filed (D02, D08, D13, D19) and were repaired; D08 was rewritten in full from a 148-line fragment. Every repair is marked in its file as repair; D02's restored harm section is marked as restoration, not recovered text.
- **Purity clean across the set.** No probabilistic, fitted, learned, similarity, or statistical machinery is adopted anywhere; no tuned constant is presented as ruled anywhere.
- **One outright outcome contradiction in the whole run:** D18's reproduction R1 — on "He does crush the old man," the ruled system REFUSEs (ω) and D18 HOLDs. D18 names this itself as its invariant-10 bend against ruled C-41 [JONATHAN]. There is no second contradiction of that kind.

**Alder's read of the core:** the convergence is evidence about the *record*, not about any dossier.
